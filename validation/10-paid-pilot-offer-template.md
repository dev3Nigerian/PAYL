# Merchant MCP Commerce Sandbox Pilot

Customize every bracketed field using the merchant interview.

## Objective

PAYL and [Merchant] will test whether authorized consumer AI agents can discover [selected offers], receive a signed quote, pay with a single-use PAYL mandate, and create an accurate merchant order through MCP.

The four-week sandbox uses simulated funds.

## Scope

- One merchant tenant and sandbox MCP endpoint.
- [Number] offers from one product/service category.
- Structured catalog, price, availability, fulfillment, cancellation, and refund rules.
- Signed quote, order commit, fulfillment status, receipt, and refund tools.
- Up to [number] invited consumer testers.
- One PAYL reference consumer AI client and wallet MCP server.
- Weekly technical and commercial review.

## PAYL provides

- Merchant MCP gateway and schemas.
- Catalog adapter support.
- Consumer sandbox wallets and policies.
- Signed payment mandates and simulated settlement.
- Approval, replay protection, ledger, receipts, and audit evidence.
- Integration support and pilot dashboard.

## Merchant provides

- Commercial and technical owners.
- Sandbox catalog and deterministic pricing.
- Fulfillment and refund state definitions.
- Sandbox order handler or agreed gateway adapter.
- Staff time for testing and weekly review.
- Permission to use anonymized pilot performance data.
- Final commercial decision meeting.

## Success criteria

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Time to first valid signed quote | Under 1 working day after catalog connection |
| Completed sandbox orders | At least [number] |
| Supported-flow completion | At least 95% |
| Quote/payment/order mismatch | 0 |
| Replayed or duplicate captures/refunds | 0 |
| Order fulfillment accuracy | 100% |
| Complete merchant and consumer receipts | 100% |

## Limitations

- No real money in the sandbox.
- No public consumer access.
- No reuse of payment mandates.
- No raw consumer payment credential supplied to the merchant or AI model.
- Live operation requires partner, legal, security, privacy, KYC/KYB, settlement, refund, and incident approval.
